How to delete your account

How to delete your Atlas account from the app or by email, and what data is erased in each case.

If you want to stop using Atlas 64 or close your account, you can do it in two ways: directly from the app, or by emailing us from the address you signed up with.

Option 1: Delete your account from the app

  1. Open Atlas 64 on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Find the Delete my account option, or Account and then Delete account.
  4. Confirm the action when asked.
  5. The deletion is processed immediately.

Important: once confirmed, the data linked to the account is erased and the account cannot be recovered afterwards.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you don’t have the app installed or can’t reach the settings screen, send an email from the address you signed up with to:

admin@wololabs.com

In the subject line, write: Account deletion.

We will reply as set out in the privacy policy, and the deletion is processed within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting the account erases, among other things, this data:

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Password hash
  • Country and avatar
  • Device identifier
  • Unlocked games linked to the account

Data erased from your phone

If you uninstall the app, the data stored only on the device is erased along with it. This includes:

  • Statistics and play time
  • Last game opened
  • Signed-in session

Need help?

If you have questions or the request wasn’t completed, email us at admin@wololabs.com and we’ll help you sort it out.
