1. Safety warnings

Choking hazard — small parts

The Atlas board includes 8 generic pieces with interchangeable bases and the Atlas Core module. The pieces, their bases and any detachable components are small parts that can cause choking if put in the mouth.

  • Minimum recommended age: 14. Not suitable for children under 3.
  • Keep the product, its parts and its packaging out of reach of babies, young children and pets.
  • Minors must always use the product under the direct supervision of an adult.
  • Before and after each game, check for loose, broken or chipped pieces. Remove any damaged piece immediately.
  • If the product includes magnets: swallowing two or more magnets, or a magnet together with a metal object, can cause intestinal perforation and can be fatal. Seek emergency medical care immediately.
  • Throw away plastic bags, film and other packaging materials before giving the product to a minor: they are not toys and present a choking hazard.

Battery — risk of fire, burns and injury

  • Do not puncture, crush, cut, bend, disassemble, incinerate or immerse the product or its batteries. Lithium polymer cells are especially sensitive to puncture and pressure: internal damage can cause fire or explosion, even some time after the impact.
  • Do not expose it to fire, flames, ovens, microwaves, prolonged direct sunlight or temperatures outside the operating range of 5 °C to 35 °C (41 °F to 95 °F) (for example, inside a closed car in the sun).
  • If you notice swelling, deformation, overheating, an unusual smell, smoke, noise or leaking fluid: stop using the product immediately, unplug it if you can do so safely, move it away from flammable materials and contact us. Never charge it again.
  • If battery contents come into contact with skin or eyes, rinse with plenty of water for 15 minutes and seek medical attention.
  • The battery is internal, rechargeable and non-removable: it cannot be replaced by the user. Do not try to remove it or open the device to reach it.
  • The Atlas board and its accessories contain no removable batteries, button cells or coin cells. If you ever find a loose battery in the packaging, do not use it and contact us.

General warnings

  • The Atlas board is an indoor electronic product. Do not use it in the rain, outdoors, or in humid, wet or very dusty environments, or where there are flammable vapors or a risk of explosion.
  • Do not place it on unstable surfaces or up high, where it could fall on someone.
  • Do not use it while driving, operating machinery or doing anything that requires your attention.
  • Do not leave cables where they could cause tripping or strangulation.
  • Stop using the product if it shows visible damage, frayed cables, a cracked casing or abnormal behavior.

The box includes:

  • Atlas Core

4. Battery and charging

The board contains two rechargeable lithium polymer (LiPo) batteries of 3500 mAh and 3.7 V each, internal and non-removable, with a total rated energy of approximately 25.9 Wh. To charge it safely:

  1. The board charges through its USB-C port, with the supplied cable. Use only that cable and a certified power adapter that meets the safety standards of your country, with a 5 V output. Using unsuitable chargers can cause fire, electric shock or permanent damage, and voids the warranty.
  2. Charge in a dry, ventilated place, on a non-flammable surface and within the range of 5 °C to 35 °C (41 °F to 95 °F). Charging outside that range damages the battery and can be dangerous.
  3. Do not charge the product unattended, while you sleep, or covered by blankets, pillows, clothing or paper.
  4. Unplug the charger when charging is complete and during electrical storms.
  5. Do not charge a product that is wet, damaged, deformed or that has suffered a hard knock or fall.
  6. If you won’t use the board for a long time, charge it to about 50 % and repeat every 3 months to preserve the battery.

Battery life and capacity are estimates and vary with use, temperature, age and number of charge cycles. Gradual battery degradation is normal wear and is not a defect covered by the warranty.

5. Radio signals and interference

The Atlas board transmits and receives radio frequency signals to communicate with the app.

Implanted medical devices

Radio signals can interfere with pacemakers, implantable defibrillators, insulin pumps, hearing aids, cochlear implants and other medical devices.

  • Keep a minimum distance of 15 cm (6 inches) between the board and any implanted medical device.
  • If you suspect the product is interfering with a medical device, turn it off immediately.
  • If you use a medical device, consult your doctor or the device manufacturer before using the board. We cannot assess compatibility with your particular device.

In addition:

  • Turn off the board wherever the use of radio equipment is prohibited by signs: aircraft, hospitals and healthcare facilities, gas stations, fuel or chemical depots, and blasting areas.
  • The product may experience interference from other equipment (routers, microwaves, other Bluetooth devices) that causes disconnections or delays. This is not a defect.
  • Connection range is approximate and depends on the environment, obstacles and the phone used.
  • We do not guarantee an uninterrupted or error-free connection.

6. Lights, sound and health

Photosensitivity warning

A small percentage of people may experience dizziness, altered vision, muscle twitching, disorientation, involuntary movements, seizures or loss of consciousness when exposed to flashing lights or light patterns, even with no history of epilepsy.

Stop using the product immediately and consult a doctor if you experience any of these symptoms. If you have a history of epilepsy or seizures, consult your doctor before using it. Minors are especially susceptible: supervise them.

  • Do not stare directly at the light emitters or point them at the eyes of other people or animals, especially at close range.
  • Play in a well-lit room, at a comfortable distance from the board, and take 10 to 15 minute breaks every hour.
  • If the product emits sound, keep the volume at a moderate level. Prolonged exposure to high volume can damage hearing.
  • Stop using it if you feel eye strain, a headache or discomfort in your hands, wrists, arms, neck or shoulders. If the discomfort persists, consult a healthcare professional.

7. Getting started

  1. Take the board and its accessories out of the packaging and check them for shipping damage.
  2. Fully charge the board before first use, following the Battery and charging section.
  3. Install the AtlasHub app from Google Play and create your account.
  4. Turn on Bluetooth on your phone and grant the permissions the app requests. Android requires the location permission to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices; as explained in the privacy policy, the app does not access your location.
  5. Turn on the board and follow the pairing wizard in the app.
  6. Pick a game and start playing.

8. Everyday use and cleaning

  • Place the board on a flat, stable, dry and clear surface.
  • Do not put heavy objects on the board, sit or stand on it, or use it to hold other objects.
  • Do not force the pieces or the connectors. Insert them the right way round and without excessive pressure.
  • Turn off the board when you are not using it.
  • Cleaning: turn off and unplug the product. Use a soft cloth, dry or slightly dampened with water. Never immerse the board or use alcohol, solvents, abrasives, compressed air or harsh cleaning products. Let it dry completely before turning it on or charging it.
  • If liquid gets into the product, do not turn it on or charge it: contact us.

9. Storage and transport

  • Store it in a dry, cool, ventilated place, within the range of 5 °C to 35 °C (41 °F to 95 °F), away from direct sunlight and heat sources.
  • Store it out of reach of young children and pets.
  • Air travel: products with lithium batteries are subject to restrictions. The board must travel in your carry-on baggage, never in checked baggage, turned off and protected against accidental activation. Its total rated energy (25.9 Wh) is below the 100 Wh threshold above which the carrier’s prior approval is required, but always check the rules of your airline and aviation authority before flying: conditions may vary.
  • Protect the product from knocks and vibration during transport. Damage caused by improper transport is not covered by the warranty.

10. Troubleshooting

Symptom What to do
The board won’t turn on Charge it for at least 30 minutes with the original cable and try again.
The app can’t find the board Check that Bluetooth is on, that permissions are granted and that the board is on and close to the phone.
The connection drops Move the phone closer, move other wireless devices away and restart the app.
The battery runs down quickly Charge it fully, avoid extreme temperatures and check for a firmware update.
Noise, smell, heat or deformation Stop using it immediately, do not charge it and contact us.

If the problem persists, write to us at admin@wololabs.com with the model, the serial number and a description of the fault.
