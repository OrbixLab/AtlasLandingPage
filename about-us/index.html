02. Our story

How we
started

We love board games, but we also know their friction points: complex rules, long setups and chores that get in the way of actually playing.

That’s why we created Atlas: a system that combines the magic of physical play with technology to make the experience simpler.

A single board that can bring hundreds of different worlds to life.

03. Our mission

Modernizing the way we play without losing what makes board games special

Atlas technology works quietly: it simplifies the rules, speeds up setup and deepens immersion without stealing the spotlight.

It all comes down to one idea: making play the only thing that matters again.

Our vision is to create a new category of hybrid games and an ecosystem where players and creators can discover and build new experiences.

More than principles: the ideas that guide every decision we make.

  1. Technology should disappear.

    We believe the best technology is the kind that works quietly. It simplifies the experience without interrupting it and lets all the attention go back to the game.

    1

  2. The best worlds don’t exist yet.

    Atlas was designed to keep evolving. Every new game, expansion or community creation can open a door to a completely different universe.

    2

  3. Every game deserves to evolve.

    We don’t believe in products that are finished once they leave the box. We believe in platforms that grow with the people who use them.

    3

  4. Curiosity always finds a way.

    Exploring, discovering and experimenting are part of Atlas’s DNA. We design tools so every player can find new ways to play, create and share.

    4

4.0 Our philosophy

We design experiences,
not just products.

Innovation
We look for new ways to reinvent the play experience without losing its essence.
Simplicity
We strip away complexity so the experience feels natural from the very first minute.
Community
Atlas grows with the people who play, create and share new worlds.
Accessibility
We want discovering new games to keep getting easier for everyone.
Excellence
We design a system built to last through thousands of games, for many years.
