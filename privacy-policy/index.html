Privacy policy

AtlasHub

What data we handle, what we use it for and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This is a translation. The Spanish version is the reference text.

AtlasHub is an app by wololabs that brings together board games to play with the physical Atlas board. This policy explains what data we handle, what for, and how to ask us to delete it.

We wrote this to be understood. If anything is unclear, email us at admin@wololabs.com.

1. Who we are

The data controller is wololabs, the developer of AtlasHub. Contact: admin@wololabs.com.

2. What data we collect

Your account data

When you sign up, we store:

  • Email address: to identify you when you sign in and to send you a recovery link if you forget your password.
  • Username: identifies you within the app.
  • Password: we don’t store it. We store a hash — a cryptographic result from which the original password cannot be recovered. Nobody at wololabs can read your password, not even with access to the database.
  • Country and chosen avatar: to display your profile.
  • Device identifier: a code we generate to link your account to your phone. It is not the IMEI, the serial number or an advertising identifier, and it can’t be used to recognize you outside AtlasHub.
  • Account creation date.
  • Which games you have unlocked, so they are available if you reinstall.

Data that stays only on your phone

This is not sent to any server and disappears if you uninstall the app:

  • Your play time and game statistics.
  • The last game you opened.
  • Your signed-in session.

What we DON’T collect

  • We don’t use analytics, advertising or trackers. The app includes no third-party SDKs of that kind.
  • We don’t collect your location.
  • We don’t sell or share personal data with third parties. Ever.
  • We don’t access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

3. What we use the data for

Only to make the app work:

Data Purpose
Email and password Sign in and recover access
Username, country, avatar Display your profile
Device identifier Link the account to your phone
Unlocked games Give you back your games when you reinstall

We don’t use your data to profile you or for advertising, and we don’t make automated decisions about you.

4. Permissions the app requests

Bluetooth

AtlasHub communicates with the physical Atlas board via Bluetooth. Game data travels between your phone and the board in front of you: it doesn’t go through our servers.

Location

Android has historically required the location permission for an app to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices. It is an operating system requirement, not our decision.

AtlasHub does not access, store or transmit your location. The permission exists only so the phone lets us find the board.

Internet

To sign in and sync your unlocked games.

5. Who we share data with

We don’t sell data. We share it only with the providers needed for the service to work:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — hosts our servers and database, in the us-east-1 region (United States).
  • Google (Gmail) — sends password recovery emails.
  • Google Play — distributes the app. Google handles download data under its own policies.

We may also disclose data if legally required to do so by a competent authority.

6. Where it is stored and international transfers

Data is stored on AWS servers in the United States. If you use the app from another country, your data is transferred there. The connection between the app and our servers is always encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

7. How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. If you delete it, we erase your personal data within 30 days at most.

Password recovery links expire and are discarded automatically.

8. How to delete your account and your data

From the app, in Settings → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate: your email, username, password hash, country, avatar, device identifier and unlocked games are erased. There is no grace period and no way to recover the account afterwards.

Without the app installed, email us at admin@wololabs.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Account deletion”. We process it within 30 days.

Data stored on your phone is erased when you uninstall the app.

You also have the right to ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to withdraw your consent. Write to the same address.

9. Children

AtlasHub is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we detect an account belonging to a child under 13, we delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has created an account, contact us.

10. Security

  • Passwords are stored as hashes, never in plain text.
  • All traffic between the app and the servers is encrypted with TLS.
  • The database is not reachable from the internet: only our application server can access it.
  • Recovery links are also stored as hashes and expire.

No system is infallible, but we take these measures seriously.

11. The atlas-64.com website and cookies

Everything above refers to the app. On this website we measure visits to learn which pages and sections are viewed most, how people navigate and what needs improving. We use two tools for this:

  • Google Analytics — counts visits, where they come from (a search engine, a social network, an AI, another site), which sections are viewed and which buttons are clicked, for example “Sign up now”.
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymously records how the page is browsed (movements, clicks and scrolling) to build heat maps and see which parts are understood and which aren’t. It does not record what is typed into forms.

We ask before using cookies. The first time you visit, a notice lets you accept or reject them:

  • If you accept, Google Analytics and Clarity store cookies in your browser to recognize a returning visit and follow the path through the site.
  • If you reject them or haven’t chosen yet, no cookies are stored and Clarity is not loaded. Google Analytics only receives an anonymous count of the visit, with nothing that identifies you or recognizes you later.

We don’t know who you are: we don’t see names or email addresses, we don’t match this data with your AtlasHub account and we don’t use it for advertising. Google and Microsoft process it under their own policies.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we update this page and the date above. If the change is significant, we notify you in the app.

13. Contact

wololabs — admin@wololabs.com
