2. What AtlasHub is

2.1 AtlasHub is the phone app where Atlas 64 games run. It connects to the console over Bluetooth and lets you:

  • browse your library and the store, install games and download 3D models to print;
  • connect the board modules and see their status;
  • play alone, against another person or in multiplayer, including on your local network;
  • create and manage your account;
  • update the console’s firmware;
  • if you are a creator, test on your phone the games you make with Atlas Studio (developer mode).

2.2 Games are played between your phone and the board in front of you. The App needs an internet connection to sign in, download games and sync your library.

2.3 The App is free. Today all available games are free and there are no in-app purchases.
